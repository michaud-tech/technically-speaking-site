The 5-minute assessment

Make the case. See how it lands.

You'll get a short brief: your role and situation, the person across the table, the facts on hand, and a decision that needs to move.

Then you write the pitch you'd actually make. We score it on the TECH Model — the same rubric we use with participants — and show you where it landed.

Takes about five minutes. Nothing is saved.
