How it works

Real work.
Live practice.

01

Design

We learn what's happening in your business, who your team needs to influence and the pushback they're likely to face. Then we design the course and exercises around it.

02

Baseline

Before we start, all participants complete a baseline assessment against the TECH rubric.

03

Practice

Participants bring their proposals, client recommendations, executive updates and stakeholder conversations into the room. They practice pitches aimed at their stakeholders, handle real objections live and get expert coaching throughout.

04

Pitch

The final session puts it together with a pitch to the sponsor of the program.

The six weeks

Learn one skill at a time. Then put it all together.

Week 1

Target Audience

Connect the pitch to what matters to the audience and the business.

Week 2

End Goal

Decide what you want and practice asking for it clearly.

Week 3

Clarity

Make complex work easy to follow and its impact obvious.

Week 4

How You Say It

Use pace, volume, tone and pauses so the important parts land.

Week 5

Draft and feedback on a real pitch

Apply the full model to a conversation already happening at work.

Week 6

Live pitch and pushback handling

Deliver the pitch, respond to objections and adapt in the moment.

The shared language keeps paying off.

Clearer communication creates value in two places.

Client work

More opportunities move forward.

  • Clients understand the value of the recommendation.
  • Strong ideas are less likely to be lost because they were explained poorly.
  • Teams spend less time revisiting work clients misunderstood.

Internal work

Decisions happen faster.

  • Teams spend less time reworking misunderstood ideas.
  • Leaders spend less time translating work for everyone else.
  • Good ideas reach the people who can act on them in language they value.
Better results get noticed.
