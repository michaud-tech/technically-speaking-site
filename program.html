The method

It's built on the TECH Model.

A shared way to think through any message, pitch or conversation before it happens.

TTarget Audience

Who am I talking to? What matters to them? What matters to the business?

From "What do I need to explain?" to "Why does this matter to them?"

EEnd Goal

What am I trying to make happen?

From sharing information to knowing the decision you need, and asking for it clearly.

CClarity

What do they actually need to know?

From jargon, acronyms and edge cases to stories and metaphors your audience gets.

HHow You Say It

How am I perceived?

The public-speaking piece: pace, tone, pauses and how you earn attention.

And then we add pushback.

Participants practice what to do when someone disagrees, challenges the recommendation or simply says no. The goal is learning how to listen, find the real concern and adapt in the moment.
